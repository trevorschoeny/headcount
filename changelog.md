## 1.0.0

First release.

### Added

- A count under the crosshair of how many entities are crammed together.
- Scroll to select, which steps through crammed entities with the mouse wheel and sends your next click to the picked one.
- Range counts, up to 16, shown on the HUD. They need Headcount on the server.

Requires MenuKit 7.3.0 or newer.
